## Cleaning tally

Every dropped row has a reason; rows in minus dropped equals rows out for every source.

- **Medical Appointment No Shows** — dropped: negative age: 1; age above 110: 5; scheduled after the visit day: 5. 'No-show' renamed to 'attended' and inverted (No -> True). 81 neighbourhoods mapped to 22 fictional clinics.
- **Disease Symptom Prediction** — dropped: exact duplicate row: 4,616. melted 304 unique rows to 321 (condition, symptom) pairs.
- **Medical Transcriptions** — dropped: empty transcription: 33; not a patient-care document (autopsy): 8; duplicate note (kept the most specific label): 2,611. 38 source labels mapped to 31 specialties plus document types.
- **A-Z Medicine Dataset of India** — dropped: exact duplicate row: 5; price not positive: 4. pack size parsed by regex for 100.00% of rows. 11562 distinct compositions.
- **Healthcare Dataset (synthetic)** — dropped: exact duplicate row: 534. billing, insurance, room and source doctor columns dropped: out of scope (p4). 39876 source hospital names mapped to 6 network hospitals.
